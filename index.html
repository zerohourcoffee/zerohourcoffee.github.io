Neo Switch Hybrid

An advanced immersion-and-percolation brew on the Hario Switch — built for clarity up front and a syrupy, sweet finish. Dial grind to taste; placeholder recipe, swap in your own.

Water

Ratio

Temp

Total time
3:00

Setup

  • 16 g light-roast, single origin
  • 256 g water at 94 °C
  • Hario Switch + tabbed filter
  • Grind: medium-fine (filter)
  • Gooseneck kettle & scale/timer
  • Switch down = closed · up = open

Method

  1. Rinse & prep. Rinse the filter, discard water, add grounds, level the bed. Start with the switch closed (down).
  2. Bloom — 0:00. Pour 50 g, swirl gently to saturate. Let it steep closed for 45 s.
  3. Immersion pour — 0:45. Pour steadily to 160 g total, keeping the switch closed. Give one gentle swirl.
  4. Open & percolate — 1:15. Flip the switch open and let it begin draining as you pour.
  5. Final pulses — to 256 g. Add water in two pulses up to 256 g by 1:45, pouring in slow circles.
  6. Drawdown. Leave switch open; aim for the bed to run dry near 3:00. Swirl once, serve, and taste as it cools.
